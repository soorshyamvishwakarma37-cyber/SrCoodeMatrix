1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SR CodeMatrix Pvt Ltd ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores and shares your personal information when you use our website and services. We are committed to processing data lawfully, fairly and transparently in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (as amended) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India, and other applicable regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated automatically when you use the website:

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored only via Firebase Authentication), display name.
  • Order data: billing name, email, phone, state/UT, optional buyer GSTIN, items ordered and payment reference IDs.
  • Seller data: business name, category, phone, email and plan information.
  • Communications: messages sent through contact forms, newsletters and support requests.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/device type, pages visited and referring URLs, collected via cookies/localStorage and Google Analytics (measurement ID configured for this site).
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Razorpay. We receive only the payment ID, order ID, status and masked metadata — we never see or store full card numbers or UPI credentials.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data to:

  • Process orders, deliver digital products and issue GST invoices;
  • Manage seller onboarding, payouts and marketplace operations;
  • Send transactional emails (invoices, order status, account notices) and, with your consent, marketing newsletters;
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax and consumer-protection laws;
  • Improve the website, analyse usage trends and personalise your experience.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of: your consent (for newsletters and optional cookies); performance of a contract (to fulfil orders and seller agreements); compliance with legal obligations (GST invoicing, record-keeping); and our legitimate interests in operating a safe, efficient marketplace.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with:

  • Razorpay — to process payments (governed by their privacy policy);
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics — for hosting, authentication and analytics;
  • The relevant seller — limited order details required to fulfil your purchase;
  • Law enforcement or regulators — when required by law, court order or to protect our rights;
  • Service providers who assist us under confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Retention

Order and invoice records are retained for at least 7 years as required under GST and tax laws. Account data is retained until the account is deleted. Newsletter data is retained until you unsubscribe. Technical logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes. When data is no longer required, it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and browser localStorage to keep you signed in, remember your cart, remember preferences (such as dismissed banners) and measure traffic. You can clear cookies/storage through your browser settings at any time; some features (cart, login) will then reset.

8. Data Security

We employ industry-standard measures including HTTPS encryption in transit, Firebase Authentication for account security, role-restricted administrative access, and input validation across the platform. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we therefore also limit the personal data we collect to what is strictly necessary.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements);
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time (unsubscribe link in every email);
  • Grievance redressal by contacting our grievance officer (below).

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

11. International Transfers

Data may be processed on servers operated by Google (Firebase, Analytics) and Razorpay, which may be located outside India. Such transfers are conducted in accordance with applicable Indian law and appropriate safeguards.

12. Third-Party Links

The website may contain affiliate links (Amazon, Flipkart) and links to seller websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites. We encourage you to review their policies.

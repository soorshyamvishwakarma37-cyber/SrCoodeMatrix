1. Scope

This Delivery Policy applies to all purchases made on SR CodeMatrix Pvt Ltd. Our marketplace currently supplies digital products and services only — no physical goods are shipped. Accordingly, no courier or postal delivery is involved.

2. Digital Product Delivery

Delivery methods by product type:

  • Downloads / Licence keys: made available immediately on the order confirmation screen and by email to your registered address.
  • Courses / SaaS access: access instructions or invite links are emailed within a few minutes of successful payment.
  • Templates / Files: secure download links are shared by email; links remain valid for the period stated on the product page.
  • If an email does not arrive within 30 minutes, check spam/junk, then contact support with your Order ID.

3. Service Delivery Timelines

Professional services (development, design, integration) follow the timeline agreed at order placement or in the subsequent statement of work. Typical starts:

  • Initial contact/acknowledgement: within 24 working hours;
  • Project kick-off: within 2–5 business days of payment, depending on scope;
  • Milestone deliveries as per the mutually agreed schedule.
  • Delays caused by delayed client inputs will extend timelines accordingly.

4. Delivery Confirmation

An order is deemed delivered when: (a) the digital product email/download has been sent to your registered email; or (b) the service milestone has been submitted to you; or (c) you confirm receipt, whichever is earliest. A GST invoice is issued simultaneously.

5. Failed or Delayed Delivery

If you experience a delivery failure:

  • Contact support@srcodematrix.com or phone +91 78699 69190 with your Order ID within 7 days;
  • We will re-issue access, repair the issue, or — where delivery is impossible — process a refund under our Refund Policy;
  • We are not liable for delays caused by incorrect email addresses, email provider outages or force majeure events.

6. Territorial Restrictions

Digital products are delivered globally unless a product page states regional restrictions (e.g. India-only GST invoicing or licensing). It is your responsibility to ensure the product is permitted in your jurisdiction.

7. Lost Access & Re-delivery

If you lose access to a delivered product (expired link, changed email address, deleted account):

  • Contact support with your Order ID and the original registered email within 12 months of purchase;
  • After verifying ownership, we will re-issue download links or access credentials free of charge;
  • Licence keys already redeemed with the publisher cannot be re-issued — contact the publisher directly in such cases.

8. Delays Beyond Our Control (Force Majeure)

We are not liable for delivery delays caused by events beyond reasonable control, including internet or power outages, payment-gateway downtime, government restrictions, natural disasters, cyber-attacks or pandemics. In such cases we will extend timelines by the duration of the disruption and keep you informed by email.

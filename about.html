About the Company

SR CodeMatrix Pvt Ltd

A software company born in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh — that now builds products and gives makers a platform to sell them. Our marketplace brings software, development services, courses and templates together in one place — with verified sellers, secure Razorpay payments and real human support.

We believe small creators and studios rarely get a fair shot on big platforms. That's why our pricing is transparent, our payouts are fast and our dashboard stays simple.

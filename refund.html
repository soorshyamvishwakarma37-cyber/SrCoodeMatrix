When and how refunds and cancellations are processed.

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when and how refunds are issued for purchases made on SR CodeMatrix Pvt Ltd. By purchasing on our marketplace you agree to this policy, which is read together with our Terms & Conditions. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

2. Digital Products (Software, Templates, Courses, Licences)

Because digital goods are delivered instantly, refund eligibility applies when:

  • The product is technically defective, corrupted, or cannot be accessed/downloaded;
  • The product materially differs from its description and the seller cannot remedy it within 7 days;
  • Duplicate payment or an erroneous charge has occurred.
  • Refund window: requests must be raised within 7 days of the payment date.
  • After 7 days, or once a licence key has been redeemed/downloaded and works as described, the sale is final.

3. Services (Development, Design, Integration)

Service orders follow milestone-based refunds:

  • Before work starts: full refund minus any gateway charges;
  • After work starts: refund of the unearned portion for milestones not yet delivered;
  • Once a milestone is delivered and accepted, that milestone amount is non-refundable;
  • Where an NDA or custom development has begun, refundability is assessed case-by-case in good faith.

4. Subscriptions & Memberships

Seller plans (Growth/Enterprise) and Pro Buyer membership are billed in advance. You may cancel anytime; the plan remains active until the end of the paid billing period and does not renew after cancellation. Except where required by law or where a billing error occurred, fees already paid for a started period are non-refundable.

5. Promotions, Tips & Add-ons

Featured-placement fees, checkout add-ons (priority support, extended cover) and voluntary tips are non-refundable once the service window or delivery has begun, unless the paid service was not provided at all.

6. Cancellation by Us

We may cancel or refund an order if: payment is suspected fraudulent; the seller is unable to deliver; a pricing error is identified before fulfilment; or the order violates our policies. In such cases the full amount is refunded to the original payment method.

7. How to Request a Refund

Email support@srcodematrix.com or use the contact form with: (1) Order ID or Razorpay Payment ID; (2) registered email; (3) reason and screenshots if applicable. Our team reviews requests within 2 business days and may contact the seller for verification.

8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method via Razorpay. Typical timelines:

  • UPI / Wallets: 1–3 business days;
  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5–7 business days (bank dependent);
  • Net Banking: 5–7 business days.
  • Refund status can be tracked with the Razorpay refund reference shared by email. Gateway charges may be deducted where permitted by regulation.

9. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback without contacting us first, we may temporarily suspend your account while we investigate. We encourage you to reach out — most issues are resolved faster through our support team.
